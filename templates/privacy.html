Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects the information you provide directly to us, including your email address for account registration and any financial data (expenses, categories, amounts) that you manually log into the application.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide you with personal expense tracking services, including generating spending summaries, category breakdowns, and managing your user account. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. However, you acknowledge that no electronic transmission or storage method is entirely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not currently share your data with any third-party services. If we implement third-party integrations in the future, we will update this policy and notify you accordingly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
